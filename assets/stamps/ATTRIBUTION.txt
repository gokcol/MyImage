MyImage bundles 160 selected graphics from Twemoji v16.0.1. SVG originals
are included in the source tree; the PNG files are rasterized at 512 x 512
with transparent backgrounds. No Snagit artwork is included.
The category/name mapping is recorded in catalog.json and src/stamp_catalog.rs.
