New in MyImage 2.0:

- One image, one project: captures, imports, pastes and new canvases automatically become .myimage projects containing the original image and editable changes.
- Automatic project saving, with pending changes saved before switching images or quitting. Recents and Library show projects only; PNG/JPEG and other flattened files are explicit exports.
- Existing capture/project pairs are consolidated by matching original pixels. Verified old captures in dated MyImage storage can move to Trash; external imported originals remain untouched. Deleting a project also includes its embedded image and any remaining linked capture in MyImage storage.
- Pearl Compact Capture Now with a large red circular Capture button, lighter graphite background and compact options.
- In-place text and callouts, visual Magnify styles, quick image effects, layers and presets.
- Compact Settings, selectable GUI/icon/pointer styles, the complete Calico pointer family, a resizable properties panel and Pen in the main toolbar.
- Improved background/reopen behavior and optional start at login, off by default.
- Lighter, compact arrow and shape controls with icon-based styles, a vertical stroke-weight slider and popups positioned away from the selected drawing.
